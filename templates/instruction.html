How to Use!

Search for a medication using its product name (eg. for paracetamol, search panadol instead) in the search bar. The product name can usually be found at the back of the blister pack!

If you cant find it, you can type the product name/active ingredient and enter, bringing you to a list of medication with that active ingredient/name

Side effects are just POSSIBLE side effects that you should look out for in case it happens, and most are rare please do not be afraid.

Contraindications is for those who should not be taking the medication or seek medical advice from a professional healthcare individual first.

NOTE: The information provided on this website is for general informational purposes only and is NOT INTENDED AS MEDICAL ADVICE.

Dosages may vary between individuals (due to age, weight, medical conditions, and other medications). If you have any doubts or queries, please CONSULT A QUALIFIED HEALTHCARE PROFESSIONAL.
